FAQ

Where is the event taking place?
Kazakh-British Technical University, Tole-bi street 59, Almaty. The entrance is from Abylaykhan street side.
What is the ICPC?
The International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC) is an annual multi-tiered competitive programming competition among universities worldwide.
Who is eligible to participate?
The competition is held both onsite and online — everyone is welcome to participate!
How many people should be in a team?
Teams consist of a maximum of three people.
What kind of problems will we solve?
Problems are similar to those found on Codeforces. For an example, see: codeforces.com/problemset/problem/4/A.
Who prepared the problems?
All problems were prepared by KBTU students and alumni.
